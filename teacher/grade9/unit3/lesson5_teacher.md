# Lesson 5 Teacher Guide
## Grade 9 ICT – Unit 3
### Emerging Technologies and the Future of Networking

---

# Lesson Overview

This lesson introduces students to modern networking technologies that are changing how people communicate, store data, and connect devices. Students will explore cloud computing, the Internet of Things (IoT), edge computing, artificial intelligence in networking, and 5G technology.

Duration: 60 minutes

---

# Learning Objectives

Students will be able to:

- Explain cloud computing.
- Describe IoT devices and their applications.
- Compare cloud computing and edge computing.
- Explain how AI can improve network management.
- Describe the benefits of 5G networks.
- Discuss future careers involving networking technologies.

---

# Prior Knowledge

Students should already understand:

- LAN and WAN
- Internet protocols
- Client-server model
- Cybersecurity basics

---

# Teaching Notes

## Cloud Computing

Emphasise that cloud computing stores data on remote servers rather than only on a local computer.

Examples:

- Google Drive
- Microsoft OneDrive
- Dropbox

---

## Internet of Things

Students should recognise everyday IoT devices such as:

- Smart watches
- Smart TVs
- Smart lights
- Smart speakers

Discuss how these devices exchange data through the Internet.

---

## Edge Computing

Explain that edge computing processes data closer to where it is generated.

Advantages:

- Lower latency
- Faster response
- Reduced network traffic

---

## Artificial Intelligence

Examples include:

- Automatic threat detection
- Network traffic optimisation
- Predictive maintenance
- Spam filtering

---

## 5G

Discuss:

- Higher bandwidth
- Lower latency
- Massive IoT support

Applications:

- Smart cities
- Self-driving vehicles
- Remote healthcare

---

# Common Misconceptions

Students may think:

- The cloud is "in the sky."
- AI replaces all network engineers.
- 5G only makes phones faster.

Correct these misconceptions with real-world examples.

---

# Discussion Questions

1. Why is cloud computing popular?
2. Which IoT devices do you use?
3. Why is edge computing important?
4. How can AI improve security?
5. How might 5G change everyday life?

---

# Formative Assessment

Observe students during discussions.

Check that students can:

- Identify cloud services.
- Explain IoT.
- Compare cloud and edge computing.
- Give examples of AI in networking.

---

# Extension Activity

Students research one future networking technology and prepare a one-minute presentation.

Possible topics:

- 6G
- Quantum networking
- Smart cities
- Digital twins

---

# Homework

Research one smart device in your home or community.

Describe:

- How it connects to the Internet.
- What data it collects.
- How it improves daily life.

---

# Teacher Reflection

After the lesson, consider:

- Which concepts were most difficult?
- Did students distinguish cloud and edge computing?
- Were students able to connect networking concepts with real-life technology?
