# Lesson 4 Teacher Guide

## Overview
Students learn the importance of cybersecurity, the CIA triad, encryption, common cyber threats, and safe online behaviour.

## Learning Outcomes
- Explain cybersecurity.
- Describe confidentiality, integrity and availability.
- Recognise common cyber threats.
- Explain why HTTPS is secure.

## Lesson Flow
1. Starter (5 min): Discuss online accounts students use.
2. Teach (20 min): Cybersecurity concepts and CIA triad.
3. Activity (10 min): Identify threats in scenarios.
4. Review (5 min): Exit questions.

## Key Vocabulary
- Cybersecurity
- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability
- Encryption
- Malware
- Phishing
- Firewall
- HTTPS

## Assessment
Observe discussion, workbook completion, and quiz responses.
