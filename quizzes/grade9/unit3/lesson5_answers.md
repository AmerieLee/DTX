# Part B. Short Answer

### 6. Explain one benefit of cloud computing.

Sample answer:

- Files can be accessed from anywhere with an Internet connection.
- Data can be backed up automatically.
- Multiple users can collaborate easily.

Award 2 marks for one clear, correct explanation.

---

### 7. Give two examples of IoT devices.

Possible answers:

- Smart watch
- Smart speaker
- Smart TV
- Smart light
- Smart thermostat
- Security camera

Award 1 mark for each correct example.

---

### 8. Why is edge computing useful?

Sample answer:

Edge computing processes data close to where it is created, reducing latency and improving response time.

Award:

- 1 mark for mentioning local processing.
- 1 mark for mentioning reduced latency or faster response.

# Part C. Extended Response

### 9. Which future networking technology will have the greatest impact?

Suggested marking guide (4 marks)

4 marks
- Clear opinion
- At least two accurate supporting reasons
- Well-organised explanation
- Correct ICT terminology

3 marks
- Good explanation with two relevant ideas

2 marks
- Basic explanation with one supporting reason

1 mark
- Limited response

0 marks
- No relevant answer

Example response:

I think Artificial Intelligence will have the greatest impact because it can automatically detect cyber attacks and optimise network traffic. AI can also reduce human error and improve the reliability of future networks.
