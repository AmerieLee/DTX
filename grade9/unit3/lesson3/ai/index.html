    AI Lab

    Unit 3 – Networks and the Internet

    Lesson 3 – Exploring Network Protocols with AI

    Lab Overview

    Artificial Intelligence (AI) can explain complex ideas, answer questions, and provide examples.

    In this lab, you will use AI to help you understand network protocols. You will also learn how to write clear prompts, check the accuracy of AI responses, and improve AI-generated explanations.

    Remember:

    AI is a learning assistant, not a replacement for your own thinking.

    Learning Objectives

    By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

    • write effective prompts
    • ask AI for clear explanations
    • compare AI responses with your textbook
    • identify inaccurate or incomplete information
    • improve AI-generated answers
    • explain network protocols in your own words

    Responsible Use of AI

    When using AI:

    ✅ Ask clear questions.

    ✅ Check information using reliable sources.

    ✅ Think critically about every answer.

    ✅ Rewrite ideas in your own words.

    Never:

    ❌ Copy AI responses without understanding them.

    ❌ Assume every AI response is completely correct.

    ❌ Submit AI-generated work as your own without following your teacher's instructions.

    Before You Begin

    Think about these questions.

    1. What is a network protocol?
    1. What is the difference between TCP and IP?
    1. Why is HTTPS important?

    These questions will help you compare your own understanding with AI explanations.

    Prompt Writing Tips

    A good prompt usually includes:

    • the audience
    • the topic
    • the level of detail
    • the desired format
    • any examples you want

    Example:

    Explain TCP to a Grade 9 student in about 120 words.

Use simple English and one real-life example.

    Before using AI for schoolwork, ask yourself:

    ☐ Did I write my own prompt?

    If you answered Yes to all five questions, you are using AI responsibly.

    Lesson Summary

    Today you learned that AI is most useful when you:

    • ask clear questions
    • check every answer carefully
    • compare information with reliable sources
    • improve AI-generated explanations
    • think critically rather than copying responses

    You also strengthened your understanding of network protocols, including:

    • DNS
    • IP
    • TCP
    • HTTP
    • HTTPS

    These technologies work together to make communication on the Internet reliable and secure.
